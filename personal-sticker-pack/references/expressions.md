# Expression lists

Twelve per pack. Swap freely; these are starting points that covered most of a real chat week.

## Cute (no text)

01 waving hello · 02 thumbs up · 03 finger heart / hand heart · 04 palms together, bowing thank you · 05 hugging a pillow, good night · 06 jumping with joy · 07 shy, blushing · 08 eating from a bowl · 09 head tilt, question mark · 10 waving a little flag, cheering · 11 teary eyes · 12 OK hand sign

## Funny (no text)

01 jaw drops to the floor · 02 facepalm · 03 laughing so hard, slapping the table · 04 soul floating out of the mouth · 05 suspicious side-eye · 06 shrug · 07 dramatic faint · 08 so angry, steam from the head · 09 dollar signs in the eyes · 10 pointing at the camera · 11 nervous, sweating buckets · 12 mind blown, head exploding

## Relatable (one line of text each)

Write the text in the language the user actually chats in. Keep it to 2 to 6 characters / 1 to 3 words.

| # | Scene | Text (中文 example) | Text (English example) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | deadpan salute | 收到 | Noted |

| 03 | holding a stack of papers, tired | 又要改？ | Revise again? |

| 06 | night, coffee, staring at screen | 加班中 | Overtime |

## Couple / duo (no text, both people in every sticker)

01 hug · 02 making one big heart together · 03 kiss on the cheek, other one blushes · 04 standing apart, arms crossed, pouting (quarrel) · 05 one angry with hands on hips, the other kneeling and begging · 06 eating together · 07 under one blanket, good night · 08 clinging to the other's arm · 09 walking hand in hand · 10 working side by side at one laptop · 11 thinking of the other (the other appears in a thought bubble, not beside them) · 12 both cheering, arms up

For duo packs, give each person their own outfit that pairs with the other's (e.g. navy hoodie and mustard hoodie), never the same outfit.
