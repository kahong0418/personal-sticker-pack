# Style shortlist

Picked for one test: shrunk to a chat-sized sticker, can a friend still tell it is you? Styles that turn everyone into the same generic face (rage comics, Wojak, stick figures) and styles where the material steals the show (crochet, plush, balloon) are left out for that reason.

Each entry has a **style line** to paste into the brief.

## Tested (used for real packs)

### Cute · Japanese Chibi ｜ 可爱 · 日系 Q 版
Big head, small body (about 1:1.5), round face, enlarged eyes, soft colours. Identity markers (hair, glasses, dimples) become even more visible when the head is big.
> Style line: `Japanese chibi sticker style, big head small body (about 1:1.5), round face, large expressive eyes, soft pastel shading, clean outlines, white die-cut sticker border, transparent background`

Best for: daily chat, couple packs.

### Funny · Exaggerated Meme Cartoon ｜ 搞笑 · 夸张 Meme 漫画
Real facial features kept, expressions pushed to the extreme: jaw on the floor, steam from the head, soul leaving the body.
> Style line: `exaggerated meme cartoon sticker, keep the person's real facial features, extreme comedic expression and distortion, bold ink outlines, flat bright colours, comic effect marks, white die-cut sticker border, transparent background`

Best for: reactions, group chats.

### Relatable · Korean Webtoon ｜ 共鸣 · 韩漫
Close to real proportions, clean lines, each sticker a small everyday scene with one line of text ("Received", "Overtime", "Client says will consider").
> Style line: `Korean webtoon style sticker, near-realistic proportions, clean line art, soft cel shading, small everyday scene with minimal props, one line of bold handwritten-style text with thick outline, white die-cut sticker border, transparent background`

Best for: work chats, "this is so me" moments. The only style that carries text.

## Good alternatives (not yet tested here)

| Style | Style line | Use instead of |
|---|---|---|
| Korean Kawaii ｜ 韩系治愈 | `Korean kawaii sticker, simple soft lines, muted warm palette, cosy everyday mood, white die-cut border, transparent background` | Chibi, if the user wants calmer and warmer |
| Squash & Stretch ｜ 橡皮脸 | `squash and stretch cartoon sticker, rubbery elastic face and body, exaggerated deformation, bold outlines, white die-cut border, transparent background` | Meme cartoon, if they want more physical comedy |
| Deadpan ｜ 面瘫冷幽默 | `deadpan humour sticker, expressionless face, minimal flat colours, white die-cut border, transparent background` | Webtoon, for dry humour |
| Semi-realistic cartoon ｜ 半写实卡通 | `semi-realistic cartoon portrait sticker, faithful facial features, moderately exaggerated expression, bold ink outlines, thick white die-cut border, transparent background` | Any, when likeness matters most |
| 3D animated ｜ 3D 动画人物 | `3D animated character sticker, rounded stylised modelling, soft studio lighting, expressive face, white die-cut border, transparent background` | Chibi, for a toy-like look |

## Rules for any style

- Keep the identity markers in every image, however stylised.
- One outfit per person across the pack.
- Brand colour, if the user has one, only on small props or accents. A whole sticker tinted in one colour looks like an ad.
