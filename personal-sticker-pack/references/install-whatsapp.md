# Adding the pack to WhatsApp

WhatsApp cannot open a zip of stickers by itself. Pick one route. App names and button labels change between versions; if a step looks different on the user's phone, follow what the screen shows.

## Route A · Separate packs with their own icon (recommended)

1. Send the zip to your phone (AirDrop, Telegram to yourself, Google Drive). On iPhone, tap it in the Files app to unzip.
2. Install a sticker maker app. Search "Sticker Maker" in the App Store / Play Store; the well-known one is free with ads and in-app purchases.
3. Create a new pack, give it a name, add the PNGs from the `png` folder (some apps add one image per slot), and set the tray icon to `tray.png`.
4. Tap **Add to WhatsApp**. Repeat for each pack.

## Route B · No extra app

1. Save the PNGs to your Photos / Gallery.
2. In a WhatsApp chat, open the sticker panel and look for the option to create a sticker from a photo, then pick one image at a time.
3. These go into your own created-stickers list, without a pack name or icon. WhatsApp may run its own cut-out on the image.

## WhatsApp's sticker rules (the pack script checks these)

- 3 to 30 stickers per pack
- each sticker 512×512 WebP, under 100KB
- tray icon 96×96 PNG, under 50KB
